## Field Descriptions

- **Business:** Business name.
- **Type:** Pub, restaurant, café, barber, retail, etc.
- **Contact Date:** Date of the initial visit.
- **Contact Person / Role:** Person spoken to and their role, if known.
- **Google Rating:** Google rating at the time of the initial visit.
- **Reviews:** Number of Google reviews at the time of the initial visit.
- **NFC Currently?:** Whether the business already uses NFC for reviews.
- **Review Process:** How the business currently asks customers for reviews.
- **Initial Interest:** Initial interest in the product, rated from 1 to 5.
- **Pilot?:** Whether they agreed to the one-week trial.
- **Notes:** Relevant observations or comments.

### Initial Interest Scale

- **1:** Not interested
- **2:** Low interest
- **3:** Moderate interest
- **4:** High interest
- **5:** Very high interest
