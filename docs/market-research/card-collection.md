# Card Collection & Pilot Feedback

## Objective

The goal of the second visit is to evaluate the one-week pilot and determine whether the NFC cards provided value to the business.

The person you meet may not be the same person who received the cards, so the questions should be simple enough to answer based on the experience of the business during the trial.

---

## 1. Introduction

> Hi, I left some NFC review cards here about a week ago for a trial. I'm just here to collect them and get some quick feedback on how they worked.

If the person is not aware of the trial:

> No problem. They were left here as part of a small one-week test. I just need to collect them and ask a couple of quick questions about whether you noticed them being used.

## 5. Pricing & SaaS Interest

Only ask pricing questions if the business shows genuine interest in continuing with the product.

### Question 11

> If you decided to keep using it, what would you consider a reasonable monthly price for this service?

**Expected monthly price:** €________ / month

Then explain the intended model:

> The idea I'm considering is a small monthly subscription, probably around €5–10 per month. This would include the NFC cards, tap tracking and access to statistics about how and when the cards are being used.

> The system would also continue to evolve over time. As the project develops, I plan to add more statistics and insights about how the cards are being used, with the goal of helping businesses understand what works best to encourage more customers to leave reviews.

### Question 12

> How does that sound to you?

**Price perception:** ___ / 5

**Scale:**

- 1 = Far too expensive
- 2 = Expensive
- 3 = Reasonable
- 4 = Good value
- 5 = Very good value
