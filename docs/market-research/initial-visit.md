# Initial Visit & Pilot Introduction

## Objective

The goal of the first visit is to:

* Understand how the business currently obtains Google reviews.
* Identify potential problems with their current process.
* Evaluate how important Google reviews are to the business.
* Introduce the NFC review card.
* Offer a one-week free trial.
* Collect enough information to evaluate the potential value of the product.

The conversation should feel natural rather than like a formal interview.
