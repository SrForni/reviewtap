# Market Research

This directory contains the documentation and data collected during the initial market validation of ReviewTap.

## Objective

The goal of this phase is to determine whether local businesses see value in using NFC cards to make it easier for customers to leave Google reviews.

The research is focused on understanding:

* How businesses currently obtain Google reviews.
* How important Google reviews are to them.
* What problems exist in their current review process.
* Whether NFC cards are perceived as useful.
* Whether businesses are willing to test the product.
* Whether the product provides noticeable value during a one-week pilot.
* Whether businesses would consider paying for the service.
* Which features could make the service more valuable over time.

## Research Process

The initial validation process consists of two visits.

### 1. Initial Visit

The business is introduced to the project and asked a short series of questions about its current review process.

If appropriate, one or two NFC cards are provided free of charge for a one-week trial.

See:

`initial-visit.md`

### 2. One-Week Pilot

The business uses the NFC cards for approximately one week.

Each card has a unique Card ID so that its usage can be tracked and associated with the correct business.

### 3. Card Collection

After approximately one week, the cards are collected and the business is asked about its experience.

The objective is to understand:

* Whether the cards were used.
* Whether staff interacted with them.
* Whether customers understood how to use them.
* Whether the business noticed any impact on reviews.
* Whether the cards provided value.
* Whether the business would continue using them.
* Whether a small monthly subscription would be considered reasonable.

See:

`card-collection.md`

## Data Collection

Business-level information and card assignments are recorded in:

`prospects.md`

The research uses a combination of:

* Structured 1–5 ratings.
* Multiple-choice responses.
* Objective measurements.
* Qualitative notes.

This allows businesses to be compared while still preserving useful feedback from individual conversations.

## Pilot Philosophy

The objective is not to prove that the product works.

The objective is to find out whether it actually provides value to businesses.

Negative feedback, lack of interest and failed pilots are considered valuable results because they help identify problems with the product or its positioning.

## Future Analysis

Once enough businesses have been contacted, the collected data can be analyzed to identify patterns such as:

* Which types of businesses show the most interest.
* Whether businesses with more Google reviews perceive greater value.
* Whether businesses already using QR codes are interested in NFC.
* Whether the cards are actually used during the pilot.
* Whether usage correlates with increased review activity.
* What price businesses consider reasonable.
* Which statistics or features could provide additional value.

The research process and product should evolve based on these findings.
